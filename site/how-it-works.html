How it works

Three steps, no tabbing away.

Hintlyx works live during your conversation — not after it. Here's the whole flow.

01

It listens

Hintlyx captures the conversation from your system audio and mic, and transcribes it live with Whisper. Silence and filler are trimmed automatically.

02

It finds the question

It pulls the exact question the other person asked out of the chatter and surfaces it as a one-click suggestion — the newest one first.

03

You get the answer

Click it or press Tab, and a clear, human answer streams into your overlay in seconds, grounded in the conversation and your screen.

Listen

It hears both sides of the room.

System audio and your microphone are captured together, so Hintlyx follows the whole conversation — not just you. The latest question is always front and centre.

See

It reads what's on your screen.

One shortcut captures your screen and sends it to a vision model. Ask about a coding problem, a chart, a spreadsheet or a slide, and get an answer grounded in exactly what you're looking at.

Answer

It talks like a person, not a report.

Answers stream in clean formatting — bold key points, bullets, highlighted code — written in a natural voice you can say out loud. Pick a mode for interviews, meetings, lectures or coding.
