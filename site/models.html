Google Gemini

Smart routing

Fast first, reliable always.

For a typed question, Hintlyx goes to Groq for the quickest possible answer. For anything involving your screen, it uses Gemini's vision. If a provider is rate-limited or down, it retries and falls back automatically — you just see the answer.

Smart mode switches to a stronger model when you want depth over speed, and the offline fallback picks up when the internet doesn't.
