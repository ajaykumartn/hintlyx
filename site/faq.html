FAQ

Good to know.

The quick answers. Anything missing? It's all in the open on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. Hintlyx is free and open source (MIT). You bring your own free API keys from Groq and Google Gemini, so there's no subscription from us.

Is my data private?

Everything runs on your PC. Your keys are encrypted locally. Audio and screen text are sent only to the AI provider you choose, and nowhere else. There is no Hintlyx server.

What does "invisible" mean?

The overlay is hidden from screen-sharing, recording and screenshot tools on Windows 10 (2004) and newer — you see it, viewers don't, including the mouse pointer over it. Use it only where that's allowed.

Do I need to set anything up?

Install, then paste two free API keys into Settings (links are provided in the app). That's the whole setup, about a minute.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes, with downloads for Apple Silicon and Intel. System-audio capture on Mac needs an extra step today; the microphone works out of the box. On first run, right-click the app and choose Open.

Will it update itself?

On Windows, yes — it checks for new versions and updates in the background. Install once and you're set. On macOS you update by downloading new versions.

Can my company use it?

Absolutely. Point it at your company AI keys, or reach out about a team setup with central management and support.

Is it detectable in interviews or exams?

Hintlyx is a visible assistant on your own screen. Only use it where AI assistance is permitted — it's built for meetings, lectures and interview practice.
