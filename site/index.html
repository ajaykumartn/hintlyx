Free. Bring your own API keys. Windows 10 & 11, 64-bit.

How it works

  1. 1 It hears the other person through your system audio and your mic.
  2. 2 It pulls out the exact question being asked.
  3. 3 You click it (or press Tab) and the answer streams into your overlay.

What you get

Invisible on screen share

Hidden from screen sharing, recordings and screenshots, including the mouse pointer. Windows 10 (2004) and newer.

Under three seconds

Streamed answers from Groq, with Google Gemini as a fallback. First words usually in about a second.

Reads your screen

One shortcut captures your screen and answers what is on it — code, questions, documents.

Live transcript

Accurate transcription with Whisper, cleaned up, with the latest question surfaced for you to answer.

Private by default

Runs on your PC. Your API keys are encrypted locally. Nothing is sent anywhere except the AI providers you choose.

Works offline

Optional local fallback through Ollama, so answers keep coming if your internet drops.
