Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A focused set of tools that make Hintlyx fast, private and genuinely useful in the moment.

Invisible on screen share

Hidden from screen sharing, recordings and screenshots — even the mouse pointer. Starts hidden every launch.

Under three seconds

Streamed answers from Groq, with Google Gemini as a fallback. First words usually in about a second.

Reads your screen

One shortcut captures your screen and answers what's on it — code, questions, documents, slides.

Live transcript

Accurate transcription with Whisper, cleaned up, with the latest question highlighted for you.

Private by default

Runs on your machine. Your API keys are encrypted locally. Nothing leaves except to the AI you choose.

Works offline

Optional local fallback through Ollama keeps answers coming even when your internet drops.

Session history

Every conversation is saved locally — revisit the transcript and the answers whenever you need them.

Global shortcuts

Ask, grab your screen, show or hide — all from the keyboard, without leaving your current app.

Auto-updates

Install once. Hintlyx keeps itself current in the background, so you're always on the latest build.

Private by design

Your conversations never become someone else's data.

  • Runs entirely on your own computer — there's no Hintlyx server.
  • API keys are encrypted at rest with your operating system's secure storage.
  • Audio and screen context go only to the AI provider you choose, over your own account.
  • Sessions are stored locally and you can delete any of them in one click.
  • Open source and MIT licensed — anyone can read exactly what it does.
